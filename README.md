# Bio-Rad Laboratories (bio-rad-laboratories)
Bio-Rad Laboratories is a global leader in developing and manufacturing a wide range of products for the life science research and clinical diagnostic markets, including instruments, reagents, consumables, software, and services. Founded in 1952, Bio-Rad serves scientists in genomics, proteomics, cell biology, quality control, and clinical diagnostics with products like real-time PCR systems, western blotting instruments, flow cytometers, and digital PCR.
